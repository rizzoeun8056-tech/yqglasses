Inside our work.

Production, office and showroom scenes from YQ GLASSES. Workshop images lead this photo collection.

One connected route
from idea to order.

We operate our own processing and assembly line and coordinate specialist resources for mold development, gift-box packaging and logistics. This gives each project a clear path from drawing to sample and standardized batch production.

Logo details and coordinated tabletop presentation can be considered during the design review. Champagne glasses and cake serving sets are among the categories we work with; the production plan is defined by the selected design.

From drawing
to delivery.

A clear path keeps each custom project moving.

  1. Your drawing

    Share a sketch, reference image or technical drawing.

  2. Custom mold

    We coordinate tooling and a sample where the design requires it.

  3. Production

    Approved materials and components move into batch work.

  4. Assembly

    Experienced staff bring the selected parts together.

  5. Inspection

    Finished pieces are checked against the approved sample.

  6. Packing

    Protective and gift-box packing are arranged for the order.

  7. Shipping

    We coordinate handover with the logistics provider.

The people behind
every detail.

More than 30 experienced assembly staff work within a company team of over 50. Our office and showroom keep communication, sample review and production coordination close together.

We work with buyers in Europe, the United States, the Middle East, Africa, South America and selected markets in Oceania. Requirements for each destination are reviewed with the order.

Design patents.

We can help document custom product designs and coordinate design patent applications. A written contract can define ownership; where eligible, we can arrange a transfer of patent rights to the client through the required registration process. Protecting distinctive designs can help reinforce your brand's position as an innovator.
