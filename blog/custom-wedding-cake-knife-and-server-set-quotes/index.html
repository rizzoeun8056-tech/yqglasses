Compare quotes for a custom wedding cake knife and server set by first aligning product specifications, order quantities, and delivery terms, then comparing total costs. Looking only at unit price can hide differences in set contents, customization, packaging, and shipping charges.

Why Isn't the Lowest Unit Price Always the Best Choice?

The lowest unit price does not necessarily mean the lowest total order cost, because quotes may cover different product configurations, packaging, customization, or delivery terms. Ask every supplier to quote the same specifications and quantity, then compare the total cost with all included charges.

What Product Details Should a Quote Specify?

A quote should specify the set contents, materials, dimensions, finish, packaging, and pricing unit. Without these details, you cannot confirm whether different suppliers are quoting the same product.

What Exactly Is Included in the Set?

Confirm every product and accessory in each set, such as one cake knife, one cake server, and whether a gift box, insert, or protective packaging is included. Accessories shown in product photos may not be included in the quote, so ask the supplier to list them individually.

How Should You Match Product Specifications?

Compare the material and grade, dimensions, blade style, server shape, handle material, and finish. If a specification matters to your purchase, ask every supplier to quote to the same standard and record it in the quotation.

If the products will contact food, ask the supplier for material information and relevant documents for the intended sales or use market. Do not judge compliance from broad descriptions such as "food grade" alone.

What Unit Is the Price Based On?

Confirm whether the unit price is per piece, per set, or per box, and make sure the order quantity uses the same unit. If one set contains multiple pieces, do not compare a per-piece quote directly with a per-set quote.

How Do Order Quantity and MOQ Affect the Quote?

Different order quantities may have different unit prices, so ask suppliers to quote for your actual requirement and list the applicable quantity tiers. Also confirm whether the minimum order quantity (MOQ) is calculated per set, style, color, or package.

If you plan to order multiple colors or styles, ask whether they can be combined toward the MOQ and whether each style has a separate minimum.

What Customization Costs Should You Check?

Ask suppliers to itemize charges for engraving, logos, surface finishes, and custom packaging. If plates, molds, or samples are involved, confirm whether those charges are one-time costs or apply to every order.

When submitting a logo or personalized wording, provide the final artwork or text and confirm its position, size, color, and application method. If names or wedding dates vary across products, also confirm whether each item will carry different wording.

What Should You Confirm About Samples and Sample Costs?

Ask about the sample price, shipping cost, production time, and whether the sample includes the materials, engraving, finish, and packaging specified for the final order. After approving the sample, keep a record of the specifications and approval as the reference for bulk production and inspection.

If the sample will differ from mass-produced items in workmanship or packaging, ask the supplier to explain the differences in advance and confirm whether they affect the final price.

How Should You Compare Shipping and Other Charges?

Confirm the trade term and named place used for each quote, then ask who is responsible for freight, insurance, duties, and taxes. Before comparing quotes with different shipping terms, align the delivery destination and the charges included.

Estimated delivered cost = Product cost + Customization and packaging costs + Sample or sampling costs + Shipping and applicable import charges

Actual charges depend on the order and delivery terms. Use the supplier's written quotation and order agreement as the final reference.

Which Quote Details Need Further Confirmation?

Ask the supplier for clarification if the set contents, pricing unit, MOQ, customization charges, or shipping scope are unclear. Before ordering, also confirm the lead-time start point and how the approved sample will be used to verify the bulk order.

Check whether the quote includes the gift box, engraving, and shipping, and which order quantity the price applies to. This helps prevent the actual order cost from exceeding your budget.

What Information Should You Prepare Before Requesting a Quote?

Give every supplier the same product specifications, order quantity, customization requirements, and delivery destination so that the quotes can be compared. Use this inquiry template:

We plan to purchase a wedding cake knife and server set. Please quote according to the requirements below.

  • Set contents: ___
  • Materials and specifications: ___
  • Handle and finish: ___
  • Order quantity: ___
  • Engraving or logo requirements: ___
  • Gift box requirements: ___
  • Delivery destination: ___

Please specify the MOQ, unit price for the required quantity, customization and sampling costs, sample lead time, production lead time, shipping charges, trade term, and quotation validity.

Frequently Asked Questions

What Should a Quote Include?

A quote should list the product specifications, set contents, quantity and pricing unit, MOQ, customization and packaging costs, sample arrangements, lead time, and shipping terms. It should also state the quote's validity period and payment terms.

Is the Quote Per Set or Per Piece?

Use the pricing unit stated in the quotation and confirm that the order quantity uses the same unit. If a set contains multiple pieces, verify that the quoted price covers the complete set.

Can Customization Affect the MOQ?

It can, depending on the customization and the supplier's production requirements. After providing the complete customization brief, ask the supplier to confirm the corresponding MOQ, costs, and lead time in writing.

Should You Approve a Sample Before Bulk Production?

Approve a sample first if the material, finish, engraving, or packaging will affect your purchasing decision. After approval, use the confirmed specifications for the bulk order and inspection.

How Should You Compare Quotes with Different Shipping Terms?

First align the delivery destination and the charges included, then compare the estimated delivered cost. Comparing product prices alone may leave out shipping and destination-related charges.
