A few
useful answers.

What is included in a set?

One cake knife and one matching server. Dimensions, materials and packaging are confirmed for the selected design.

Can you engrave names, dates or a logo?

Yes. Send your text or artwork, preferred position and size. We confirm the layout and engraving approach for the product.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Minimums depend on the style, customization and packaging. Share your estimated quantity so we can confirm.

Can I request a sample?

Yes. Sample availability, cost and timing are confirmed for your design. New shapes may require separately quoted mold development.

Can I customize the gift box?

Yes. Gift and standard boxes support custom shape, design and wording. Materials, fit and artwork are confirmed with your order.

Are the sets dishwasher safe?

Care depends on the finish, decoration and handle construction. Confirm model-specific instructions; use gentle hand washing and prompt drying until confirmed.

What product specifications can I request?

Ask for component materials, knife and server dimensions, handle details and care instructions. Share any product-document requirements with your inquiry.

Does the lead time include shipping?

No. The ranges above cover dispatch, sampling or production. Transit is additional; include your destination and required arrival date.
