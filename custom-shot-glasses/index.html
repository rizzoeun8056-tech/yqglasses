Materials for Different Uses

Material affects clarity, weight, breakage risk and decoration compatibility. Options depend on the selected style; not every material is available with every shape or finish.

Soda-Lime Glass

A widely used clear glass option for everyday barware, events and bulk orders. Performance depends on the product design and manufacturing process.

Lead-Free Crystal

Offers a bright, refined appearance for premium gifts or branded collections. Confirm the material specification with the supplier.

Stainless Steel

A non-transparent, break-resistant option for outdoor events and travel. Logo decoration is typically selected for the metal surface.

Plastic

Lightweight and less prone to breakage, making it suitable for some large events. Confirm the exact plastic type and product-use guidance.

Material availability depends on the selected style and decoration method.

Logo Decoration Methods

The right method depends on the material, artwork detail, color count, order quantity and care requirements. Confirm compatibility for your selected product.

01 / PRINT

Screen Printing

Well suited to clear, defined artwork and solid colors. Each color may require a separate screen; availability and color limits vary.

02 / TRANSFER

Ceramic Decal

Can accommodate detailed or multicolor designs on compatible glass. The process may include firing; confirm finish and production requirements.

03 / MARK

Laser Engraving

Creates a subtle etched or frosted look on compatible surfaces. It is generally monochrome and does not reproduce a colored logo.

04 / MATTE

Sandblasting

Creates a matte, frosted design on compatible glass. Fine details and small text should be reviewed for legibility.

05 / DIGITAL

UV or Digital Print

May suit colorful artwork on selected products. Ink adhesion, durability and cleaning guidance depend on the specific system.

Vector artwork is preferred when available. File acceptance, proofing and sample options are confirmed for each project.
