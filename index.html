What products can be customized?+

Our collection covers champagne flutes, cake knife and server sets, beer glasses, shot glasses, cocktail glasses and whiskey glasses. Tell us which style you have in mind.

Can I request samples?+

We can discuss samples for selected existing designs. Availability, customization costs and freight are confirmed for each project.

What is the minimum order quantity?+

It depends on the product, materials, decoration and packaging. Share your requirements and we will recommend a suitable starting quantity.

Can you create a coordinated gift set?+

Yes. Glassware and cake-serving pieces can be brought together through matching finishes, decoration and packaging.
