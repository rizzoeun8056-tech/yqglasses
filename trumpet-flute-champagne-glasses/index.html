Care guide

Care and maintenance

  • Wash with warm water and mild detergent using a soft sponge or cloth.
  • Avoid scouring pads, abrasive cleaners and contact with hard objects.
  • Support the bowl when washing or drying. Avoid twisting the stem.
  • Avoid sudden temperature changes and dry with a soft, lint-free cloth.
  • Gently hand-wash metal-stem and rhinestone versions without prolonged soaking. Confirm care instructions for the selected finish.
  • Confirm dishwasher suitability for the specific style and finish before use.
